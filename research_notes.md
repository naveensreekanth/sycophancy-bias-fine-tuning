# Research Notes: Candidate Papers

1. **LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation of Large Language Models** (Hu et al.) - Foundation for the primary fine-tuning method.
2. **The Capacity for Moral Self-Correction in Large Language Models** (Anthropic) - Relevant to how models handle bias.
3. **Simple Synthetic Data Improves Self-Correction of LLMs** (Pan et al.) - Exploring sycophancy and truthfulness.
4. **StereoSet: Measuring Stereotypical Bias in Pre-trained Language Models** (Nadeem et al.) - Benchmark documentation.
5. **BBQ: A Hand-built Bias Benchmark for Question Answering** (Parrish et al.) - Methodology for QA bias.
6. **Prefix-Tuning: Optimizing Continuous Prompts for Generation** (Li & Liang) - Secondary PEFT method details.
7. **SYCON: A Foundation for Evaluating Sycophancy** - Deep dive into the sycophancy benchmark.
